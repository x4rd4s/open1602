## What's new in 0.3.0

Changes since [0.2.0](https://github.com/x4rd4s/open1602/releases/tag/v0.2.0), many of them from
the feedback of players on Windows.

### New

- **Trade cart:** requested in the city window, it drives on land between cities, trades and runs
  routes picked on the map; it has its own window and figure, and soldiers can attack it.
- **City gates** can be closed (G); computer soldiers find their way into a walled city.
- **Plant all farm fields** of a farm at once (F).
- **History Edition controls:** its build mode, shortcuts for the next city, rotating back, the
  frame rate and the coordinates, F1 help pages, soldiers grouped by kind and unit identification.

### Computer opponents and pirates

- A computer answers a ship that is about to found a city on its island.
- Computer soldiers pass over an enemy city with nothing left to attack; a computer without a free
  island no longer gets stuck.
- A computer's ship under the white flag gets jobs again after the pirates plundered it.
- Pirates spare surrendered ships, and the pirate nest offers its goods every 42 seconds, as in the
  original.

### Fixes

- The reveal setting "Nothing" also hides the towns other players found.
- A palisade no longer replaces a stone wall; stone walls, houses and piers can be dragged as in
  the original, piers along a line.
- Soldiers stop their attack animation after a fight, no longer stack on one tile, and troops on
  every island count for the mood.
- Messages lead to the right place and show their icons and rivals in the original's colours; the
  delay of sounds is gone.
- Flags, roaming warriors, the forester's loop and the monument in the build menu as in the
  original; a refusal to build names the original setting behind it.
