## What's new in 0.4.0

Changes since [0.3.1](https://github.com/x4rd4s/open1602/releases/tag/v0.3.1), many of them from
the third round of player feedback.

Multiplayer games need every player on 0.4.0: the network protocol has changed.

### New

- **Edge scrolling:** the map scrolls while the mouse rests at the edge of the screen (Shift for
  faster). The display window switches it on and off and has a slider for its speed (25 to 300 %).
  "Keep the mouse in the window" holds the mouse in a window too, so edge scrolling works there.
- **Mouse wheel in the menus:** the scenario and savegame lists, a mission's briefing, the mods
  list and the multiplayer lists scroll with the mouse wheel.
- **Plant fields automatically** (display window, off by default): a new farm, plantation, pasture
  or forester plants its fields as soon as it is placed, as far as the money goes.
- **More trade carts:** the city window of a market asks for a trade cart too, and the cart comes
  out of that market. Shift on the cart button sends out another cart, up to one per Kontor and
  market on the island; without Shift the button jumps from cart to cart. The game setting
  "Trade carts per island" allows only one per island instead.
- **Send out a whole castle:** Shift and a click on the castle's send-out arrow sends every trained
  unit to the next spot picked; they line up in a block around it.
- **Pirates' nests in the city list (L):** once you have discovered a nest's island, the list shows
  it with the pirates' coat of arms, and a click goes there.
- **Game speed in multiplayer:** the setting "Only the host sets speed" decides about pausing,
  resuming and changing the speed together. Without it, every player may do all three.
- **Revised bread chain by default:** new endless and multiplayer games start with the revised
  bread production. Savegames from earlier versions keep the chain they were started with.
- **Ship sales:** when another player buys one of your ships, the status bar says who bought it and
  for how much.
- **Smarter computer players:** they take up land that becomes free, build a market towards ore
  and gold deposits outside their land, lay roads up to a mine's slope, and lay piers along the
  beach where these open more land than a road.
- **Native villages:** a village that has lost its chief's house, market and huts is dissolved and
  its land becomes free. A new choice in the game settings lets the ruins keep holding the land.
- **Build tool:** ground that cannot be built on is checkered yellow, and a building you lack the
  gold or goods for blinks. The fertility view shows on Ctrl + B, and "Plant all farm fields" (F)
  also plants over forest in a farm's area.
- **`--seed` picks the map:** two new endless games with the same seed start on the same map.
- **Installing on macOS and Linux:** the README explains how to unpack the GOG installer without
  Windows.

### Fixes

- Text at resolutions below 1024 x 768 is sharp and readable instead of shrunk.
- The F1 key overview fits long texts into their columns, and only a click outside closes it. The
  attack key's label names a click on open ground too.
- The market cart button moves the view to the cart instead of the map's top left corner.
- A new market cart sets off from the middle of the Kontor and finds its way out on the coast, and
  orders to it no longer play the ships' voice.
- A ship at the pirates' Kontor can trade there as well as deal with the pirates.
- The huts of native villages in a new game recruit warriors and send them out.
- The pirates get wood and tools for their nest, so they build their hunter, fisher and towers.
- The computer players' setting is back in the "Opponents" group of the game settings.
