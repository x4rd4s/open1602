## What's new in 0.4.1

Changes since [0.4.0](https://github.com/x4rd4s/open1602/releases/tag/v0.4.0), many of them from
the fourth round of player feedback.

Multiplayer games need every player on 0.4.1: the computer players and the game settings have
changed, so 0.4.0 cannot join a 0.4.1 game.

### New

- **Fertile fields:** a new game setting in the new "Nature" group (next to droughts and
  volcanoes) lets every tile of an island carry the crops the island grows at all, as on a fully
  fertile island. An island that does not grow a crop still does not, and droughts still wither.
  Off by default; saved with the game.
- **Game speed without a keypad:** + and - on the main keys (= and - on QWERTY) change the game
  speed like Num+ and Num-, for laptops and compact keyboards.
- **Trade route editor:** a new button leads straight back to the ship's page, where the route is
  started. A right click on the panel goes back one step (from choosing a stop, from a stop's page
  to the list, from the list to the ship) and keeps the ship selected. While choosing a stop on
  the map, a right click ends the choice instead of sending the ship there.
- **Goods balance with a full warehouse:** producers that stand still only because there is no
  room left now count as production, so the balance no longer shows a shortage that is none. A
  good's page says how many producers are waiting for room.
- **City walls:** computer players wall in their cities and build gates where a wall crosses a
  road, more of them at war with a human player, and they tear down walls their city has grown
  past.
- **Taking over a city:** a ship in front of the destroyed Kontor of a defenceless city offers to
  build a Kontor of your own over it, which takes the city over.
- **Razed computer cities:** a computer player gives up a city without Kontor, market, tower and
  houses, and its land becomes free. The setting for the land of razed villages and towns lets the
  ruins keep holding it.

### Fixes

- A clicked fisher's hut keeps the water inside its circle lit, where its boat fishes.
- The switches in the building windows (production stopped, no pickup, building material kept,
  shipyard stopped) no longer flicker back for a moment after a click.
- The ship types of a small shipyard are centred together with their click areas.
- Deselecting units or ships in the military mode returns to the player status.
- An attack on a player you are already at war with no longer asks first, even with an open offer
  of peace.
- The minimap's view frame is drawn with white squares and a black border, scaled with the
  interface.
- The values of the fertile fields and trade cart settings are no longer cut off in the start
  dialog.
- The macOS packages are built on a newer build machine (macOS 11 and newer, Apple silicon and
  Intel, as before).
