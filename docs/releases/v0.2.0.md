## What's new in 0.2.0

Changes since [0.1.1](https://github.com/x4rd4s/open1602/releases/tag/v0.1.1).

### Anno 1602 History Edition

- Open1602 now runs with the data of the History Edition as well as with the GOG edition and the
  Königsedition. It finds the installation below Ubisoft Connect, in Heroic's prefixes and through
  Ubisoft's registry entries, and reads the language folder of the chosen language.
- Its main menu with the true colour art, its Game.dat and savegames in the user's folder.
- Its Bink 2 videos play (Open1602's own decoder, no new library); its Options button opens the
  display window.

### Mods

- A content registry: the values of the original's buildings and figures, buildings, goods,
  inhabitant classes and unlock levels of a package's own, all as data.
- A package can bring its own PNG pictures for buildings, figures and ships, icons for the build
  menu, goods and messages, and sounds and music as Ogg Vorbis or FLAC.
- Scripts: packages can bring a `script.lua` (Lua 5.4.8 in a sandbox) with tick events and
  messages; its state is kept in the savegame.

### Game settings

- A page "Original settings" in the start dialog brings back eleven bugs and exploits of the
  original and its computer players, one by one; Open1602's fixes stay the default.
- "Graphics: as in the original / modern" in the display window: the classic look draws shadows
  and smoke dithered as the original does.

### Playing

- Ships attack another player's buildings and Kontor with a right click; Ctrl or F attacks roads,
  bridges and piers.
- Soldiers heal at their own doctor (walk up to it, or Ctrl + left click as in the original) and
  walk onto a near place instead of stopping short.
- A destroyed Kontor keeps its city; storage and trade at destroyed Kontors as in the original.
- Shift + click on the cannons of the ship window fills every free cannon place.

### Interface

- The minimap in the original's design; a right click on the minimap no longer misfires.
- Coats of arms of the computer opponents' cities in the top bar.
- The mouse wheel turns the pages of the trade route editor and scrolls the savegames and music
  titles in the settings.
- The scenario menu shows the briefing's slider; locked campaign parts and inactive buttons are
  drawn faded; fulfilled missions play their videos full-screen, as the original.
- The endless game lists what sets the chosen level apart; the multiplayer name is remembered;
  long savegame names end in "...".

### Fixes

- After loading, a city holds no more of a good than its warehouse takes; a stored 0 in
  satisfaction and food is read as the original reads it.
- Many details checked in the game against the original: the tutorial, founding a Kontor, the
  settings dialog, the scenario list, the demolish preview and the computer opponents'
  production.
